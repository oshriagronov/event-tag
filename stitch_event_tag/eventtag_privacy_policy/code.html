On-Device Processing Guarantee

EventTag is built on a foundation of artisan-level privacy. All biometric scanning and facial recognition happens locally within your browser. We never upload your unencrypted photos to our servers for analysis.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2024

1. Introduction

At EventTag, we believe that visual memories are deeply personal. Our aesthetic blends traditional photography appreciation with cutting-edge, browser-based artificial intelligence. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your data with the utmost artisan-level care, ensuring your privacy is maintained while delivering a curated, premium experience.

By using the EventTag service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. Our primary commitment is to minimize data collection and prioritize local, on-device processing whenever technically feasible.

2. Data Collection & Local Processing

EventTag utilizes advanced AI models to curate your event galleries. We distinguish ourselves by how we process this data:

  • Biometric Data (Facial Landmarks): When you search for photos using a reference image, the extraction of facial landmarks occurs entirely within your local device's browser memory. This highly sensitive biometric data is never transmitted to, nor stored on, EventTag servers.
  • Photo Hosting: Event galleries are hosted temporarily to facilitate delivery. These images are encrypted at rest and are only accessible via secure, time-limited cryptographic links provided to event attendees.
  • Account Information: We collect standard account information necessary for service provision, including your name, email address, and billing details (processed securely via our payment partners).

3. Use of Information

The minimal data we do collect is utilized solely to provide, maintain, and improve the EventTag service. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Authenticate secure access to your curated event galleries.
  • Process transactions and deliver receipts or notifications related to your account.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries with personalized care.
  • Monitor aggregated, anonymous usage patterns to refine our interface and improve the performance of our local AI models.

4. Data Security

We employ a "defense in depth" strategy tailored for a minimalist infrastructure. Because we do not centralize biometric data, the most significant risk vector is structurally eliminated.

For data that is stored (such as account details and encrypted gallery assets), we utilize industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS for transit, AES-256 for rest). However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require further clarification regarding our privacy practices or the technical details of our on-device processing, please reach out to our privacy team.
